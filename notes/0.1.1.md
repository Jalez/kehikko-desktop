- **Issues and merge requests are read once, for every module.** Kehikot reads a project's GitHub and GitLab trackers itself, with your logged-in `gh` and `glab`, and shares what it read with every module. **Refresh all** in the bar reads them again.
- **Kehikot keeps its settings in Application Support.** What it stored in its old place is copied over on first start, and the old copy is left alone.
