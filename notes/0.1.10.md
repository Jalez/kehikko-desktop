- **Parts for every epic.** The parts control is always in the bar, and one press takes you to where parts are made.
- **Quicker part picking.** In the parts menu, the checkbox adds or removes a part, and clicking its name picks that part alone.
- **Module updates restart the module.** Updating a module restarts it when that's needed, with no extra press. **Restart to update** now always means the app.
