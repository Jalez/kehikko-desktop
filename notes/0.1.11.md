- **Modules open in your theme.** Under a light Kehikot, a module is light from its very first frame.
- **Out-of-date modules are spotted.** Kehikot notices when a module's page is older than its server, or its server older than its code.
- **Quitting cleans up.** Quitting or restarting the app stops the modules it started, and never one it didn't start or one set to keep running.
- **Faster status.** A module that stops is noticed within a moment instead of up to 30 seconds, and a long first install reads as still installing, not as failed.
