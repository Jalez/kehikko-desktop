- **Header menus.** Modules can put their views, toggles, jumps and actions into View, Go and Run menus in the container header. A Frame menu holds tools, feedback and prompts, and the header keeps its height at every width.
- **Agents can press menu items.** An agent can press the header items a module has marked for agents.
- **A stricter local server.** Kehikot answers only its own window and programs on your Mac.
