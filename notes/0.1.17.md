- **Edit an epic's parts in the picker.** Add, rename, reorder and remove parts right in the parts picker; the list is kept with the epic. Keyboard: F2 to rename, Alt+↑/↓ to move, Delete to remove.
- **Agent tools under their own names.** A module can choose the name its agent tools are connected under, and the agent tools window offers to reconnect one still connected under its old name.
- **Parts follow the modules.** When a module moves something from one part to another, the old part no longer keeps showing it.
