- **One place for updates.** The app and its modules now share one Updates menu, and one indicator in the header shows what matters most: a download in progress, **Restart to update**, or how many module updates are waiting.
- **App updates download in the background.** Kehikot checks every few hours and when your Mac wakes, downloads with a progress bar, and installs when you quit or restart.
- **GitLab fixes.** Reading particular GitLab issues and merge requests no longer fails on large projects.
- **Module updates are less fragile.** Updating a module no longer stops because an earlier install changed its files.
