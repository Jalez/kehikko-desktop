- **The Inbox.** One panel in the bar for what modules need, are doing and have finished: Next, Needs you, Working, Finished and Earlier. Press Alt+Shift+N to jump to the most pressing item across kehikot, epics and parts. It takes over from the Notifications module.
- **Pinned header buttons.** A module can pin up to four of its actions as buttons in the container header.
- **Manage tracker sources in the app.** See what each source is for and what it last read, and add, check, turn off or remove a GitHub or GitLab repository, including self-hosted GitLab.
- **Fixes.** After a restart, agents again know which kehikko you have open, and the bar shortens its text in a narrow window instead of squeezing.
