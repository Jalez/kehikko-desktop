- **All epics.** Pick **All epics** at the top of the epic picker to see a whole project at once. Factory's boards, References and Journeys show every epic's items, labelled with their epic; a module that works on one epic at a time says so instead of going blank.
- **Notices when your changes move.** The Inbox tells you when a merge or pull request is approved, when changes are requested, when your review is requested, and when someone else comments, pushes, merges or closes — one notice per item, kept up to date. Choose what you're told in **settings… → Tracker notices**.
- **More about each change.** Kehikot now reads which stack a change belongs to, who was active on it last, and the faces and names of the people on it, so References can show stacks, unaddressed activity and avatars.
