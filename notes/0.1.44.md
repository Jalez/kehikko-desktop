- **A proper welcome.** New users now meet Kehikot with a short, animated welcome: every tool for one piece of work side by side, projects that keep working when you switch, a kehikko for design, research, development, review and learning, agents that know where they are, one Inbox, and data that stays on your computer.
- **A tour that waits for you.** The first-run tour now walks you through picking a project, creating your first epic and your first kehikko, waiting while you do each one and skipping what you have already done. Tick "I've got this" at any step to pause it, and pick it up again with Continue tour in the ? menu.
- **Layouts for every way you work.** New kehikot can start from Design, Research, Development, Review or Learning layouts, alongside Write a paper, Plan an epic and Start simple.
- **What's new after an update.** After Kehikot or your modules update, one window shows what changed in each of them — once, when you're not in the middle of something. Turn it off from the window itself or in Settings › About.
