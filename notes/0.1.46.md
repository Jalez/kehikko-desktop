- **Build your own module.** Kehikot now offers a skill of its own, `/kehikot-module`, that walks you from an idea to a released module: it asks what the module is for, writes a short design for you to confirm, starts it from the official template, builds and tests it, and helps you install and release it. Find it under Settings › Skills, and the guide under Settings › About › Build your own module.
- **Nothing is written into a folder you didn't pick.** When Kehikot opens your home folder only because no project was chosen yet, it now just reads it; nothing is saved there until you pick a project.
