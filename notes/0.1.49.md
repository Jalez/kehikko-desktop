- **Every module has its own icon.** Modules now show their own icon in the modules list, What's new, Updates, Settings and their container header — Claude and Browser with their tools' own marks — and Kehikot shows its own logo instead of a sparkle.
- **The release list always tells the truth.** When Kehikot can't read its releases from GitHub, it now says so and offers Retry, keeping the last list it read, instead of claiming nothing was published.
- **Fewer requests to GitHub.** Kehikot now asks GitHub much less often: answers that haven't changed aren't fetched again, your own `gh` login is used when you're signed in, and nothing is read until a window needs it.
