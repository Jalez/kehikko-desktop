- **Every module's settings, one press away.** A gear in a container's header opens that module's settings — switches, fields and lists that save as you change them — and Settings › Modules lists every module's settings in one place. Settings that let a module act on its own, such as Factory posting or merging by itself, can only be changed by you, never by an agent.
- **Links open in the Browser on your kehikko.** When a module like References or Review opens a web page, it now opens in the Browser container on the same kehikko. If there isn't one, Kehikot asks whether to open it in your own browser or add Browser to the kehikko — nothing opens without your press.
