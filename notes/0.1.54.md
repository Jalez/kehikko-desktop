- **Calm switching.** Switching to another kehikko or project no longer makes the containers jump around: they open at their final place and size, and the old kehikko fades into the new one once it is ready. Only your own dragging, resizing and folding still animate.
- **Links open in Browser from more modules.** Journeys, Flows, Checklist, Diff and Slides now open web links in the Browser on your kehikko too, and a Browser that is still starting is waited for instead of asking you.
