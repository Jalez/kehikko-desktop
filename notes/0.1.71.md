- **Modules keep themselves up to date.** New module releases now install in the background: a module that isn't on screen updates right away, one you are looking at updates once you move away from it, and Terminal, Claude and Codex wait until their sessions end. Pinned modules, ones waiting for a newer Kehikot, and checkouts you edit are left alone. Restarting Kehikot to update it brings the module updates along. Turn it off in Settings › Modules or at the top of the Updates menu.
