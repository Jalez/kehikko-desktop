- **A chime for the Inbox.** Something new in the Inbox now plays a short chime, but never for a kehikko you're looking at, and a burst plays once. Each kind — urgent, needs you, finished, a notice — can have its own sound: one of six built-in chimes, one you compose in the chime maker, or a short sound file of your own that stays on your Mac. Choose them in Settings › Inbox sounds, and mute them from the bell in the Inbox.
- **Waiting looks alive.** Reading feedback, versions, release notes, module settings and skills now shows the loading spinner instead of words alone, and says so when GitHub is slow to answer.
