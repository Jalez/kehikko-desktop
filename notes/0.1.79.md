- **Installing modules works whatever Bun you have.** Kehikot now installs and updates modules with its own Bun, so a different Bun already on your computer no longer makes installs fail with a lockfile error.
- **Update all updates everything.** Modules that run from a folder of your own are now fetched and updated too, and any module Update all can't update is listed with the reason. The summary no longer says "up to date" while one has new commits waiting.
- **Inbox dots clear themselves.** A module that restarts without saying anything again no longer leaves its old items needing attention, and items from removed modules or deleted kehikot and epics are cleared.
