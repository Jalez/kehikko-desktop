- **Every module says what it belongs to.** A module's scope (This computer, Project, Epic, Part or Kehikko) shows in its header, in the modules menu and in its settings. Where a module supports more than one, pick it in the module's settings; it replaces the separate switches some modules had for this.
- **New Inbox items show a toast.** A short card in the corner says what arrived, with Open and Dismiss. Everything still stays in the Inbox; switch toasts off in Settings › Inbox notifications.
- **Programs started for you use your own Bun.** Agent sessions, shells and test runs that modules start for you no longer pick up Kehikot's built-in Bun, so `bun` in a hook or script behaves as in your own terminal.
