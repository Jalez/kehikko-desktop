- **Every module says what it belongs to.** A module's scope (This computer, Project, Epic, Part or Kehikko) shows in its header, in the modules menu and in its settings. Where a module supports more than one, pick it in the module's settings; Test runs, Factory and Notifications use this instead of their own switches.
- **New Inbox items show a toast.** A short card in the corner says what arrived, with Open and Dismiss. Everything still stays in the Inbox; switch toasts off in Settings › Inbox notifications.
- **Sessions use your own Bun.** Claude and Codex sessions, Terminal shells and test runs started from Kehikot no longer pick up Kehikot's built-in Bun, so `bun` in a hook or script behaves as in your own terminal.
